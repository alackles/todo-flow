# To-do repository

This repository holds Acacia's rolling to-do lists and handoffs. The live triage conversation is the actual system. These files are its residue and the memory between sessions.

## Files

- One file per working day or week, named like `week-of-oct-12-monday.md`.
- The most recent file is the current state. Read it at the start of every session.

## Structure

- **Days** use academic codes: M T W R F.
- **Blocks** within a day are named by the window, such as morning, office hours, end of day, or nap window.
- **Floating** is the undated backlog. **This week floating** is what's meant for the current week but not yet assigned to a day.
- **Size estimates** go in parentheses, like `(~1 hr)`. She pre-scopes items, so keep the estimate she gave.
- **Completed items** are marked done in the conversation and dropped from the next handoff.

## Opening a session

When she asks to surface the list:

1. Read the most recent file.
2. Present today's items, carry-forward items, and floating.
3. Group items whose status wasn't tracked under **unknown status**, not "overdue." Tracking lapses happen.
4. Ask what survived, what's dead, and what's new.

## Handoff files

Write a handoff at the end of a day or week, or whenever she asks. Write it for the next Claude instance, not for her, since she won't read it.

Include:

- what got done
- what's carried forward, with its status
- the floating lists
- any decisions made in conversation that the next session needs

Keep it terse. Use "appointment" or "unavailable" for personal and medical scheduling. Never include specifics.

## Recurring patterns

Two kinds of tasks tend to slide: self-directed admin with no one waiting on it, and deep work that needs an uninterrupted block, such as literature reviews and writing.

Don't comment on this unprompted. It's useful material when she asks for a retrospective.

## Privacy

This repository must be private. Keep student-identifying details out of it. Refer to students by role or course unless she says otherwise.
